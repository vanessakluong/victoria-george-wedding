Victoria & George

Bangkok & GP's Hometown

February 1 – 7, 2027 · Thailand

Mon Feb 1

Drive to GP's Hometown

Consider stopping at the Ayutthaya ruins en route — a UNESCO World Heritage site and only a short detour.

Tue Feb 2

Dinner with GP's Family

Explore local markets and the town in the morning. Rest in the afternoon, then dinner with the family in the evening.

Wed Feb 3

Drive Back to Bangkok

Return to Bangkok and check in. Evening: rooftop drinks — Vertigo at Banyan Tree or Octave Rooftop are both good.

Thu Feb 4

Grand Palace & River

MorningGrand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew (Emerald Buddha) — arrive early, it gets crowded and hot by midday
MiddayWat Pho (Reclining Buddha) + traditional Thai massage on-site
AfternoonChao Phraya ferry to Wat Arun (Temple of Dawn)
EveningChinatown (Yaowarat Road) — street food, open late
Fri Feb 5

Markets & Neighborhoods

MorningChatuchak Weekend Market — 15,000 stalls, one of the world's largest (open Sat–Sun; swap with Feb 6 if preferred)
AfternoonSiam Square shopping or Jim Thompson House (silk & history)
EveningAsiatique The Riverfront — night market on the river, easy to spend a few hours
Sat Feb 6

Day Trip from Bangkok

Option AAyutthaya — ancient capital ruins, ~1.5 hr by train or car. UNESCO World Heritage Site.
Option BDamnoen Saduak floating market — classic canal boat experience, ~1.5 hrs from Bangkok
Option CErawan National Park — tiered waterfalls, ~3 hr drive each way
Sun Feb 7

Fly Home from Bangkok

Suvarnabhumi (BKK) for most international routes; Don Mueang (DMK) for budget carriers. Allow 3 hours before departure. Safe travels — what an incredible week.
