Day Trip

Day Trip from Bangkok

Option AAyutthaya — ancient capital ruins, ~1.5 hr by train or car. UNESCO World Heritage Site.
Option BDamnoen Saduak floating market — classic canal boat experience, ~1.5 hrs from Bangkok
Option CErawan National Park — emerald-green tiered waterfalls, ~3 hr drive (worth it for nature lovers)
Sun Feb 7
Departure

Fly Home from Bangkok

Suvarnabhumi (BKK) for most international routes; Don Mueang (DMK) for budget carriers. Allow 3 hours before departure. Safe travels — what a week.
